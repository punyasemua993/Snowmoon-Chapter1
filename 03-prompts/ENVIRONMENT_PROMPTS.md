# Environment Prompts

## Meldan/Kalimar
Pristine green capital district, large clean stone-brick houses inspired by medieval architecture, scattered leaves and plants, small shops, dense trees, modern footpaths, understated advanced infrastructure.

## Sky Bridge
Elegant pedestrian sky bridge emerging from a rising forest path, busy road below, cars, autobuses and autonomous delivery vehicles, distant forested mountain, fifteen-storey apartment towers shaped like oversized medieval castle towers.

## Badra St #1103
Tasteful five-storey blue-grey apartment building, modern but not soulless, tree-covered sidewalks on both sides, integrated into greenery.

## Concert
Large open field enclosed by high green-and-blue soundproof barriers, sophisticated stage, mixed crowd, privacy robes and ordinary band clothing, intense concert lighting without cyberpunk excess.

## Autobus
Nearly empty clean autonomous/local bus, civic advertisements, two anonymous privacy-robed passengers, one school teenager with backpack.

## Family Home
Warm stone-and-wood interior, practical future technology, hand devices, dining table, stairs, couch, cozy evening lighting.
